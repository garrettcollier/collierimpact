01 / Our purpose

A clear mission.
A longer
view.

An impact fund built around the essentials of a better life. Our ambition is lasting community benefit, grounded in responsible investment.

Our mission

To responsibly deploy capital into essential infrastructure projects that generate sustainable financial returns and measurable improvements in people’s lives.

Our North Star

To invest long-term institutional capital into the infrastructure that protects communities, connects the world, and expands opportunity for generations.

Our intended investors

We are developing Collier Impact for university endowments, teacher and police officer pension systems, foundations, and other long-term institutional investors.

Competitive, risk-adjusted returns and measurable impact.
Our aim is to serve institutions and their beneficiaries while improving the communities our investments reach. Every potential investment must stand up to both responsibilities.

The principles we are building around

  • Patient capital
  • Financial discipline
  • Measurable impact
  • Independent governance
  • Fiduciary responsibility

02 / Our approach

Purpose, backed
by discipline.

The need is the starting point. Evidence, economics, and the ability to deliver determine what comes next.

01

Understand the need.

Listen to communities. Examine who lacks access, what households can afford, and where existing services fall short.

02

Test the economics.

Underwrite costs, cash flows, risks, and downside scenarios. Social importance is a requirement, not a substitute for a sound investment.

03

Partner to deliver.

Work with experienced investment professionals, developers, and operators. Build capabilities around a focused first strategy.

04

Measure what changes.

Define the intended benefit before investing. Track financial performance and community outcomes, including the tradeoffs.

03 / Research priorities

The essentials.
A wider lens.

Our research spans the systems communities depend on. We will select a focused first strategy through evidence, investment economics, and the right expertise.

Areas of exploration · No launch sector selected
01

Energy & resilience

Reliable power. More resilient communities.

Exploring energy and grid-support assets that can improve reliability, strengthen resilience, and make essential power more accessible.

Potential impact measuresService continuity, household energy burden, and access to reliable electricity.

02

Water & essential systems

Safe water and dependable infrastructure.

Examining drinking water, wastewater, stormwater, and resilience systems, with attention to the operating and contracting models that support lasting service.

Potential impact measuresHouseholds served, service quality, system reliability, and customer costs.

03

Digital connectivity

Connecting people to possibility.

Researching broadband and related connectivity infrastructure that can close service gaps and connect households to work, education, and opportunity.

Potential impact measuresPreviously unserved households connected, service affordability, and reliable performance.

04

Housing & communities

More attainable places to call home.

Testing approaches to attainable housing, including rehabilitation, repeatable home designs, and practical construction methods, against local household purchasing capacity.

Potential impact measuresTotal cost of ownership, access to homeownership, housing quality, and household affordability.

05

Education & public spaces

Stronger settings for learning and community.

Considering education, school safety, and community infrastructure where viable partnerships can support better, more dependable places to learn and gather.

Potential impact measuresAccess, facility condition, service continuity, and clearly defined safety improvements.

06

Mobility & logistics

Keeping communities connected.

Exploring transportation and logistics infrastructure that connects people, essential goods, and economic activity, with careful attention to scale and delivery requirements.

Potential impact measuresService access, reliability, travel or delivery costs, and connections to essential destinations.

04 / A standard for impact

Measured in
real life.

Impact should be more than a compelling story. We intend to assess what improves, for whom, and whether the benefit lasts.

Our measurement framework is in development. These are the questions shaping it.

Access

Who gains access to a service that was previously out of reach?

Affordability

What does a household pay, including ongoing costs?

Quality

Is the service dependable, safe, and useful over time?

Performance

Can the investment sustain the service and deliver competitive returns?
